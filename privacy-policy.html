Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Welcome to RankInsight (rankinsight.vercel.app). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data processed through our website and browser extension. This Privacy Policy explains how our tool operates regarding data collection, usage, and user privacy.

1 Information We Process

RankInsight is designed to be lightweight, fast, and privacy-first. Here is how we handle information:

  • URL & Page SEO Metadata: When you analyze a website or run the extension, the tool evaluates public page metadata, heading tags, keyword occurrences, and on-page performance metrics solely to display your audit report.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for, capture, or store personal information such as your passwords, financial records, or private browsing history.
  • User Preferences: Any settings (such as theme preferences or search parameters) are stored locally on your device via standard browser storage APIs (`chrome.storage` or `localStorage`).

2 Extension Permissions & API Usage

Our browser extension and web tools request only the minimal permissions required to provide SEO insights:

  • Active Tab Access: Used exclusively when you trigger the extension popup to analyze the active webpage's public structure.
  • Storage: Used to temporarily save your audit configurations or cached results on your local device.

Note: RankInsight does not track your general browsing behavior across unrelated websites, nor do we run background monitoring scripts without your direct action.

3 Data Storage & Sharing

We believe in strict zero-leakage principles:

  • No Sale of Data: We never sell, lease, or monetize user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Third-Party Hosting: Our web application is hosted on Vercel infrastructure, which complies with high industry standards for security and reliability.

4 Security of Your Data

All communication between your browser and our web application is encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. We regularly review our codebase and security configurations to ensure a safe user experience.

5 Third-Party Links

RankInsight may contain links to third-party websites or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

6 Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our service or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
